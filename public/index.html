About This Platform

Welcome to APSRTC Live Track, a real-time, interactive bus tracking and transit intelligence platform. This application tracks thousands of active buses running across all major depots in Andhra Pradesh.

Developed By

Swecha Andhra Pradesh

This tool is developed by Swecha AP, a community-driven initiative working towards building technology for society. We believe in the power of free software, open knowledge, collaboration, and community-driven innovation to create technology that empowers people and serves their needs.

Key Features

  • Pinpoint Live GPS: Streams real-time coordinates, speed, and heading angle directly from vehicle transponders.
  • Complete Route Tracing: Click any bus to inspect stop-by-stop waypoints, arrival & departure timestamps, and estimated arrival times (ETAs).
  • Smart Search & Filters: Quickly find any bus by Vehicle Registration Number, OPRS Service Number, Route Name, or filter by Depot and Service Category.
  • Seamless Background Sync: The system automatically polls for live fleet updates every 25 seconds in the background.

Status & Tracking Indicators

Live GPS Fix Vehicle transponder is active, moving, and reporting live telemetry.
Station Estimate Position calculated based on scheduled boarding stop while awaiting satellite lock.

Note: This service is an independent transit tracking initiative designed to help passengers, commuters, and transit researchers visualize live public transit data across Andhra Pradesh. This is not affiliated with APSRTC.
